## English

The Terminology chapter is the authoritative living glossary of the MenQ ecosystem. Foundation terms may be specialized in product or domain layers, but their canonical core meaning may not be changed. A new term, rename, or semantic change requires demonstrated need, bilingual semantic equality, and a traceable decision. Previous meanings are not rewritten or silently deleted.

Before work, read the root canonical files, `foundation/README.md`, `foundation/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, this folder's `README.md`, and the related decisions. A material terminology change requires Owner approval and history-preserving synchronization across every affected canonical file.

**Current state:** `Terminology v1.0` is Locked as a living standard. Related decision: `D-018`. Review triggers include ambiguity, recurring semantic conflict, a new specialization of a Foundation term, or evidence that an existing definition is insufficient.
