## Հայերեն

Owner-ը հաստատել է MenQ AI Collaboration Standard v1-ը։ Այն lock է անում Human–AI operating model-ը՝ human final authority և accountability, AI-ի `G0–G2` սահման, no self-approval, context loading և validity protocol, memory layers և isolation, explicit collaboration contract, truth/uncertainty և evidence protocol, task lifecycle, controlled execution, tool-use սահմաններ, mandatory canonical write integrity, approval և high-risk action rules, agent-to-agent delegation, handoff, failure handling, correction, security/privacy, communication, output quality, definition of done, prohibited AI behaviors, metrics և review triggers։

AI-ն չի կարող իրեն authority տալ, human approval հորինել, RED gate-ը GREEN անվանել, canonical truth-ը լուռ փոխել, failure-ը թաքցնել կամ tool success-ը verified outcome ներկայացնել։

## English

The Owner approved MenQ AI Collaboration Standard v1. It locks the Human–AI operating model: final human authority and accountability, AI limited to `G0–G2`, no self-approval, context loading and validity protocols, memory layers and isolation, an explicit collaboration contract, truth/uncertainty and evidence protocols, the task lifecycle, controlled execution, tool-use boundaries, mandatory canonical write integrity, approval and high-risk action rules, agent-to-agent delegation, handoff, failure handling, correction, security/privacy, communication, output quality, definition of done, prohibited AI behaviors, metrics, and review triggers.

AI may not grant itself authority, invent human approval, relabel a RED gate as GREEN, silently change canonical truth, hide failure, or represent tool success as a verified outcome.
